Ocean Cast

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Ocean Cast is a food inventory app. It works fully offline on your device. An account is optional, and it exists for one reason: to copy your own records between your own devices.

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing you enter leaves your device.
  • With an account, your kitchen records are stored on our server so a second device can read them.
  • Photos you attach never leave the device. Only the file name is synced.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • Deleting your account erases the server copy immediately, and the response tells you how many records were removed.

1. Who is responsible

Ocean Cast is developed and operated by an independent developer. The service described here runs at ocean-cast.space. For anything in this policy, write to support@ocean-cast.space.

If you run your own copy of the Ocean Cast server, that copy is yours: you control the data on it, and this policy describes only the service above.

2. What stays on your device

Until you create an account, the app stores everything locally in its own container: your household, storage zones, product batches, meals, reservations, shopping list, prices, activity history, downloaded recall notices and settings. Photos you attach to a product, and any receipt photo you import, are also kept locally.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is nothing to ask us for, because we never received it.

3. What is sent to the server

This happens only after you create an account and only while you are signed in.

Account

  • Email address — used to sign in and, if needed, to reach you about the service.
  • Display name — whatever you type; a nickname is fine.
  • Password — stored only as an Argon2id hash. We cannot read it or recover it.

Your kitchen records

  • Household name, currency and preferences.
  • Storage zones, and the household members you added by name.
  • Product batches: name, brand, barcode, lot code, quantity, unit, purchase and best-before dates, price, shop, notes.
  • Meals and their ingredients, reservations, shopping lines, price records, and the activity log of what you changed and when.
  • Recall notices you downloaded and the decisions you made about them.

Technical records tied to your session

  • Device name and platform, so you can recognise a session and end it.
  • Access and refresh tokens, stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • A security log of events such as sign-in, failed sign-in, password change, data export and account deletion. IP addresses in this log are stored as a keyed hash, never as the address itself.
  • Short-lived request keys that make a retried request safe, deleted after 24 hours.

4. What we never collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no social sign-in.
  • No location, no contacts, no calendar, no health data.
  • No photos. Camera frames used to read a barcode are processed live and never stored or uploaded.
  • No payment data. The app takes no payments.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you. We do not sell or share data with anyone for their own purposes.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to read a barcode you point it at. Nothing is recorded. Refusing it leaves manual entry fully usable.
  • Photo library — only for the picture you choose yourself, through the system picker. The app sees that one image and nothing else.
  • Notifications — only to remind you about a date you entered. Reminders are scheduled on the device; no reminder content is sent anywhere.

7. How the data is protected

  • All traffic runs over HTTPS. The API refuses plain HTTP.
  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id and never stored or logged in plain text.
  • Session tokens are random 256-bit values kept only as hashes on the server, and in the device Keychain on your side, marked as this-device-only.
  • Refresh tokens rotate on every use. If an old one is presented again, the whole session family is revoked and the event is logged.
  • Every request must carry a valid token, and every database query is limited to your own household.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins are rate limited, and an account locks for 15 minutes after eight failures.

No system is perfect. If you believe an account has been accessed without permission, change the password in Profile — that ends every other session — and write to us.

9. Your choices

  • Use the app without an account. Every feature except sync works offline, with no server involved.
  • Export everything. Profile gives you a full JSON export of what the server holds; Settings exports your local data as JSON or CSV.
  • Correct anything. Every record is editable in the app, and edits reach the server on the next sync.
  • End sessions. Profile lists your devices and ends any of them, or all at once.
  • Delete the account. Profile → Delete account, confirmed with your password. The account, the household and every record attached to it are removed at once, not marked hidden. You choose separately whether the local copy on the device stays or is erased.

Deletion is immediate and final. There is no recovery window and no backup we can restore from on request. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, port or erase your data, to object to processing, or to complain to your data protection authority. The controls above cover access, correction, portability and erasure directly; for anything else write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

10. Legal basis

For users in the EEA and the UK: we process account data to perform the contract you enter when you create an account, and we keep the security log on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting accounts from abuse. Without an account there is no processing on our side at all.

11. Children

Ocean Cast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the app before it takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.
